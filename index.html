One project I'm proud to share came from my 2025 summer experience with LaGuardia Community College Business Services.

2025 · Research project

New York electrical licensing guide

LaGuardia Community College · Business Services

I researched electrical licensing requirements across New York and organized my findings into an interactive PDF guide. The map lets readers select different locations and find the relevant licensing information.

The tricky part was that the requirements aren't consistent everywhere. I learned to pay attention to local rules, be clear about what I could verify, and point readers toward the appropriate authority when answers depended on the municipality.
